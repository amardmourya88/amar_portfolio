How I work

One lead funnel, five stages

Fewer people reach each stage, so I work on every one of them. The game below uses the same stages.

Stage 1Get found

SEO work: keyword research, on-page and technical fixes, audits and competitor checks.

Stage 2Get clicks

Meta Ads, Google Ads and social media posts that bring the right people to the website.

Stage 3Get leads

Responsive websites and landing pages built to make people fill the form or call.

Stage 4Follow up

WhatsApp Business API, Bulk SMS, RCS messages and cold calls to keep the lead warm.

Stage 5Measure

Google Analytics, Excel and Power BI reports to see what to repeat and what to stop.
